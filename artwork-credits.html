Artwork & credits

Artwork release 2.1.0 · 13 credited photographs used across 111 candidate entries, 34 Pokémon fan illustrations, and 16 original category illustrations. All image files ship locally and are precached for offline play.

What each image represents

Photographs of artists represent the artist, not an album cover or music video. Generic category illustrations represent a topic, not the exact candidate. Pokémon portraits are AI-generated fan illustrations, not official artwork. Their underlying characters and trademarks belong to their respective owners. No affiliation or endorsement is implied.

The 6×6 Pokémon atlas and 4×4 category atlas were generated for this project using OpenAI image generation on October 23, 2026. No official Pokémon image files, commercial album covers, film posters or Supercell assets are included.

Other game, film and anime characters currently use category illustrations rather than individual character portraits. This release does not claim complete real-image coverage.
